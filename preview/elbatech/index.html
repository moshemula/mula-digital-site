How we build

You bring the design.
We build the system.

  1. 01 · Design inputStart with a drawing

    A concept, a spec or a full build-to-print package. Elipse Engineering reviews it for manufacturability and cost before a single part is cut.

    Problems get solved on paper, not on the floor.
  2. 02 · MechanicsFrame, CNC and metal work

    Base, structure and extrusions machined and assembled in our own workshop.

    Precision parts from one accountable supplier.
  3. 03 · ElectricalCabinets built to spec

    Breakers, drives, PLC and terminals laid out, labeled and documented to your schematic.

    Every wire traceable back to the drawing.
  4. 04 · Cable & harnessEvery cable routed and tied

    Harnesses and cable trays built and inspected to IPC/WHMA-A-620.

    Clean routing that holds up for years in the field.
  5. 05 · Robotics & HLARobotics, drone, integration

    Battery-swap robot, landing lift, sensors and the drone itself integrated into one autonomous system.

    One team owns the whole system.
  6. 06 · Test & deliverTested until it flies

    Full functional test, AS9100D documentation and a real launch before the system leaves our floor.

    Ready to deploy the day it arrives.

For more than 30 years, Elbatech has grown alongside Israel's aerospace and defense industry, and alongside some of the world's leading high-tech companies. Meticulous planning, methodic execution, no-compromise quality and on-time delivery are the reasons the most demanding customers choose us.

Today the group brings together six companies, about 1,200 people and 114 active customers. With our new campus in the Ashdod Hi-Tech Park, every link of the turnkey chain will work under one roof, with shorter handoffs and one quality system.
